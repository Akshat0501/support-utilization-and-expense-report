💡 Project Coordinators BI & Analytics Overview

📌 1-Month Focused Report

  • Analyzes 1 month of project data per coordinator.
  • Per-coordinator tracking: number of projects managed and resource spend (Total Payout).
  • Bifurcated strictly by Total Resource Payout tiers (not monthly rate).

🌟 Key Findings

  • Direct placement contracts yield higher profit margins than Mediator arrangements.
  • Resource payouts categorized from below ₹1 Lakh up to above ₹2 Lakhs per project.

⚠️ Risks & Anomalies

  • Automated audit checks for negative margins, zero margins, and missing field data.

🎯 Recommendations

  • Monitor high payout projects (> ₹2 Lakhs) to optimize resource spend efficiency.
